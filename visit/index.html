Before You Come

What to bring.

Short list. We can't put a boat in the water without these. Bring them to your delivery meeting at the lake.

  • 01

    A valid driver's license

    The renter has to be 21 or older.

  • 02

    A credit card for the deposit

    A credit card authorization is required before the boat goes out. Your rental agreement sets out the security deposit and your responsibility for damage.

  • 03

    Wisconsin Boating Safety Certificate

    Required for any operator born on or after January 1, 1989. Bring it for everyone who plans to drive.

One thing to plan around. The boats can't be run from dusk to dawn. That comes from our marine insurer and we can't bend it, so build the day around daylight.

Common Questions

A few things people always ask.

What dates can we book? +

We're closed for 2026. Book May 8 through October 10, 2027. The calendar is open for those dates now. Pick your boat and your day and you're set.

How late can we stay out? +

The boats may not be operated from dusk to dawn, even within a booked rental window. That is a requirement from our marine insurer and is not something we can waive. Be tied up before dusk. We collect the boat at the end of your rental window, which is 8:30 p.m. if you took the Ranger afternoon.

Do I need any experience driving a pontoon? +

Yes. Boat operators must be at least 16 years old and have prior boating experience. Every rental starts with a driver orientation and a walk through the Wisconsin boating rules. Anyone born on or after January 1, 1989 also needs a Wisconsin Boating Safety Certificate to operate the boat. Tell us about your experience when you book.

Can I bring my dog? +

Yes, well-mannered dogs are welcome on the pontoon. You're responsible for any pet-related cleaning or damage. Cleaning beyond the usual starts at $100, so give us a heads-up and we'll put down an extra towel.

What happens if it rains? +

Light rain is fine. The canopy keeps you covered. If it turns into strong wind, lightning, or fog you can't see the next shoreline through, call us and we'll work out a new day. We'd rather lose the rental than the boat, or you.

Is there a place to dock and grab lunch? +

Places to check include Harbor View on Long Lake, Sandtrap Bar and Grill at Westwood Golf Course, Flyway Resort on Wilson Lake, and Sunset Bay or Polish Palace on Solberg Lake. Call ahead to confirm current hours and whether visiting boaters can use the dock. Plan your return before dusk.

Can we tow a tube or water-ski? +

Towing is strictly prohibited, including tubes, water skis, wakeboards, kneeboards, and other towable devices.

Do you do weddings, birthdays, or events? +

Yes. The pontoon seats 15, with a maximum weight capacity of 2,630 lbs. Whoever is driving needs to stay sober. Operating a boat under the influence is illegal in Wisconsin, and we will end a rental over it without a refund. Call, text, or email and tell us what you have in mind.
