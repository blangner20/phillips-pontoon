Short list. We can't put a boat in the water without these, whether we're delivering or you're picking up.

    A valid driver's license

  • 02

Common Questions

A few things people always ask.

What dates can we book? +

August 1 through October 15, 2026. That is our first season, and the calendar is open for those dates now. Pick your boat and your day and you're set.

How late can we stay out? +

Until dusk. The boats may not be operated from dusk to dawn, which is a requirement from our marine insurer and is not something we can waive. If we delivered the boat, standard pickup is 5:00 p.m.

Do I need any experience driving a pontoon? +

No. Every rental starts with a driver orientation and a walk through the Wisconsin boating rules, and we take the time you need. If this would be your first time on a pontoon, tell us when you book so we can plan for a longer run-through. Anyone born on or after January 1, 1989 does need a Wisconsin Boating Safety Certificate to operate the boat.

Can I bring my dog? +

Yes, well-mannered dogs are welcome on the pontoon. You're responsible for any pet-related cleaning or damage. Cleaning beyond the usual starts at $100, so give us a heads-up and we'll put down an extra towel.

What happens if it rains? +

Light rain is fine. The canopy keeps you covered. If it turns into strong wind, lightning, or fog you can't see the next shoreline through, call us and we'll work out a new day. We'd rather lose the rental than the boat, or you.

Is there a place to dock and grab lunch? +

A few. On Long Lake, Harbor View (east end) or Sandtrap Bar and Grill at Westwood Golf Course (west end). On Wilson, the Flyway resort (southwest end). On Solberg, Sunset Bay or the Polish Palace (both on the northeast side). Elk and Duroy don't have spots like that. They're stumpy, weedy fishing lakes, not cruising water.

Can we tow a tube or water-ski? +

We don't provide tubes, and nothing may be towed behind the boats unless we approve it in advance. Ask us before you book if that's part of your plan.

Do you do weddings, birthdays, or events? +

Yes. The pontoon seats 15, with a maximum weight capacity of 2,630 lbs. Whoever is driving needs to stay sober. Operating a boat under the influence is illegal in Wisconsin, and we will end a rental over it without a refund. Call, text, or email and tell us what you have in mind.
