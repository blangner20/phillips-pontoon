A walkthrough first

Every pontoon rental starts with a driver orientation. We show you the controls and how she handles before you leave the dock.

You also get a run-through of the Wisconsin boating rules. If it's your first time behind a pontoon wheel, tell us and we'll take the time you need.

Paperwork, done online

The rental agreement and waiver are signed electronically while you book. Nothing to print, nothing to sign at the dock.

Renters must be 21 or older, with a valid driver's license and a credit card. Anyone born on or after January 1, 1989 who will operate the boat needs a Wisconsin Boating Safety Certificate.
