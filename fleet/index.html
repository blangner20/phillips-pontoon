The Rates

What the day costs

Rental rates are all here. They do not include Wisconsin sales tax (5.5%), which is added at checkout. Delivery and pickup runs $80 flat to lakes within 30 miles of Phillips. Fuel, cleaning and late return fees are on the rules page.

The pontoon comes back with a full tank. The Ranger comes back at the level it went out. Boats stay off the water from dusk to dawn. That one is our insurer's rule and we hold to it.
